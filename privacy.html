cedbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how cedbot, a Discord music bot developed and maintained by ceddyyyy, handles information when you use the bot.

1. Information We Collect

cedbot does not intentionally collect or store sensitive personal information. The bot may process basic Discord information, such as your Discord user ID, server ID, channel ID, and command information, when necessary to provide its features.

2. Music Commands

When you use music commands such as c!play or c!skip, cedbot may process the command and information required to play or control music in a Discord voice channel.

3. Data Storage

cedbot does not intentionally store personal information longer than necessary to operate its features.

4. Third-Party Services

cedbot may use Discord and other third-party services to provide its functionality. Those services may have their own privacy policies governing how they handle information.

5. Data Requests

If you have questions about information processed by cedbot, you may contact ceddyyyy through Discord.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact ceddyyyy through Discord.
